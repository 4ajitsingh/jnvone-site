JNV One — Child Safety Standards

App: JNV One · Operated by Ajit Kumar Singh · Contact jnvone.app@gmail.com

JNV One is committed to the safety of children and young people who use our app. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and for child sexual abuse material (CSAM). This page describes our standards and how we act on them, in line with Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy and applicable law.

1. Prohibited conduct and content

The following are strictly forbidden on JNV One:

  • Any child sexual abuse material (CSAM), or content that sexualises, exploits, or endangers a minor.
  • Grooming, solicitation, sextortion, or any attempt to make inappropriate contact with a minor.
  • Trafficking, sexualised commentary about minors, or promoting or facilitating any of the above.

2. Minimum age

You must be at least 13 years old to use JNV One. Users under 18 should use the app only with the involvement of a parent or guardian. Accounts found to belong to children below the minimum age are removed.

3. How to report CSAE or unsafe content

We provide in-app reporting and a direct contact channel:

  • In the app: use the Report option available on posts and user content to flag anything that endangers a child.
  • By email: report to jnvone.app@gmail.com with the subject "Child safety". This inbox is monitored and reports are prioritised.

4. How we respond

  • We review reports promptly and remove violating content and accounts.
  • We ban offenders from the app.
  • We report apparent child sexual abuse material and offences to the appropriate authorities — including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and, in India, the relevant law-enforcement and cybercrime authorities — and preserve related information as required by law.

5. Legal compliance

We comply with applicable child-safety laws, including India's Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012 and the Information Technology Act and rules, and with Google Play's Child Safety Standards and CSAE policies.

6. Child safety point of contact

Child safety concerns and regulatory queries: jnvone.app@gmail.com — Ajit Kumar Singh, operator of JNV One.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services or law-enforcement authorities right away, in addition to reporting to us.

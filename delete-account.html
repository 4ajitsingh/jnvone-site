JNV One — Delete Your Account

App: JNV One · Operated by Ajit Kumar Singh · Contact jnvone.app@gmail.com

This page explains how to delete your JNV One account and what happens to your data. You can delete your account at any time, directly inside the app.

Delete your account from within the app

  1. Open JNV One and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Scroll to Account and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm when prompted. Your account and associated data are permanently deleted, and you are signed out immediately.

Prefer to request deletion by email?

If you can't access the app, email jnvone.app@gmail.com from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my account". We will verify the request and delete your account within 30 days.

What data is deleted

When you delete your account, we permanently remove:

  • Your profile (name, email, phone number, city/region, gender, date of birth, bio, headline, skills, education/work history, blood group, and profile photo)
  • Your posts, comments, likes, and bookmarks
  • Your direct messages
  • Your community memberships, event RSVPs, mentorship and election activity
  • Your push-notification token and login credentials

What may be retained

  • Content you shared that is inherently part of another member's experience (for example, a message already delivered to another user) may persist for that user, consistent with our Terms of Service.
  • Backups are purged on a rolling basis and any residual copies are deleted within 90 days.
  • We may retain a minimal record where required to comply with legal obligations or to prevent fraud and abuse.
Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone. To use JNV One again afterwards, you would need to create a new account.
